- Organize open Markdown checkboxes into one card per note across Now, Later, Someday, and Unassigned.
- Keep an individually ordered task queue alongside the note-based board.
- Complete tasks in Obsidian notes, customize lane colors, filter tasks, and hide cards from the board.
- No runtime dependencies, accounts, network requests, or telemetry.
